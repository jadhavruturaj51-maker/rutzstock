Scoreboard — RJ Scanner vs Muse Scanner vs One-Direction

The three scanners

Scanner A — RJ Scanner (original criteria)
  • Market direction decides the side (bullish = LONG only, bearish = SHORT only)
  • Volume 9:20–9:50 at least 2x the 10-day average
  • Breakout above Previous Week High and Previous Day High
  • 15m chart: price above EMA10 and EMA20
  • Stock's sector in today's top 5 sectors
  • Price above VWAP and above the first 15-min opening range
  • Gap less than 3%; stock has not already moved more than 2.5% by 9:50
Scanner B — Muse Scanner (V2-Quality + RSI)
  • Same base: market direction, Previous Day breakout, EMA10/EMA20, opening range, gap < 3%, move < 2.5%
  • Volume at least 1.0x the 10-day average (not 2x); no Previous-Week or sector top-5 hard gates
  • Quality gate: sector strength at least 1.0% in the trade direction AND VWAP distance at least 0.5%
  • RSI filter: LONG needs 15m RSI(14) at 9:50 of 80 or less; SHORT needs 20 or more
  • Ranked by sector strength + VWAP distance, max 3 per day
Scanner C — One-Direction (Scanner B + clean-trend gates)
  • Same base as Scanner B (V2-Quality + RSI), plus two extra gates — a stock is dropped, never swapped for another
  • Efficiency ratio of the 9:15–9:45 morning move at least 0.5 (clean, straight morning move — not zigzag)
  • Move by 9:50 between 0.8% and 2.0% (not under-extended, not over-extended)
  • Max 3 per day, no replacement picks
Picks are shown for each scanner, followed by the closest stocks that were not picked (dimmed rows), with the checks they failed shown in the Failed column.
