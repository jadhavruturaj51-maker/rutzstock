The two scanners

Scanner A — RJ Scanner

  • Market direction decides the side (bullish = LONG only, bearish = SHORT only)
  • Volume 9:20–9:50 at least 2x the 10-day average
  • Breakout above Previous Week High and Previous Day High
  • 15m chart: price above EMA10 and EMA20
  • Stock's sector in today's top 5 sectors
  • Price above VWAP and above the first 15-min opening range
  • Gap less than 3%; stock has not already moved more than 2.5% by 9:50
  • OI buildup and no-event criteria: not checkable from the free data source (see note)

Scanner B — Muse Scanner

  • Same base: market direction, Previous Day breakout, EMA10/EMA20, opening range, gap < 3%, move < 2.5%
  • Volume at least 1.0x the 10-day average (not 2x)
  • No Previous-Week or sector top-5 hard gates
  • VWAP distance at least 0.3% to enter the pool
  • Quality gate: sector strength at least 1.0% in the trade direction AND VWAP distance at least 0.5%
  • RSI filter: LONG needs 15m RSI(14) at 9:50 of 80 or less; SHORT needs 20 or more
  • Ranked by sector strength + VWAP distance, max 3 per day

Notes

Data source: Yahoo Finance (free). "9:50 price" is the close of the 09:45 five-minute candle. Returns are measured from the 9:50 entry to that day's close. PASSED is shown out of 12 criteria; 10 are checked from price/volume data — OI buildup and same-day events cannot be verified from the free data source, exactly as in the backtests. Days marked backtest were computed later from historical data; unmarked days are live 9:50 scans. This page is a personal research tool, not investment advice.
